N5 words written in kanji

516 N5 vocabulary items containing kanji, cut into five tiers by how far the reading follows from the characters, then ordered inside each tier the way a kanji dictionary is: grouped under a shared head character, with each new group chosen so it reuses characters you have already passed. A word marked free introduces no new character at all — you can already read it. 毎日 costs two characters; by the time you reach 来月 and 来週 they cost nothing.

出現順

Kanji by yield

Sorted by how many N5 words use them. Blue is on'yomi — the reading that carries across compounds; grey below it is kun'yomi. 日 turns up in 25 words, then it falls off a cliff: 260 of the 417 appear in exactly one. Open any character to see its words in learning order, colour-dotted by tier.

    How the order is built. Inside a tier, words are grouped by their first kanji — all 毎〜 together, like a jukugo dictionary. Within a group, the word whose second character has the widest reach comes first. Group order is then chosen greedily: at each step the next head character is the one giving the most words for the fewest unseen characters, so 毎 (introducing 日週月晩年) is followed by 来 and 今, whose members are then almost all free. Nothing is sorted at random.
    How the tiers are built. Each reading was matched character by character against that character's on'yomi and kun'yomi from KANJIDIC, allowing regular rendaku (か→が) and gemination (く→っ). Tier 1 requires every character to use its first-listed on'yomi with no sound change. Tier 4 catches native counter readings; Tier 5 is everything whose reading refuses to decompose.
    Sources. Word list: Tanos unofficial JLPT N5 vocabulary (700 entries) via the Bluskyo CSV conversion. Glosses: JMdict [2026-08-30]. Readings, strokes, character-frequency ranks: KANJIDIC [2026-242]. Read offline from your local Yomitan copies.
    Honest limits. The JPDB frequency dictionary was dropped — it ranks kana spellings, so がくせい lands 152,000th because 学生 is almost never written in kana. Counts of "free" words differ between the compounds-only and full lists, because what counts as already-seen depends on which list you are walking. Four Tanos errors patched: 明い→明るい; とり肉 and さ来年 had no entry at that spelling; 見る was stored merged with 観る.
