01 / The Engine

Two heuristics, one output
Both of the moves you rely on are excellent. Both push in the same direction. That shared direction is the whole finding.
H1 — INVERSION
What would have to be true about someone for this behavior to be normal and reasonable?
Strong at
Dissolving false hostility. Recovering the frame someone is actually operating inside.
Structural flaw
It will generate an equally good answer for the opposite behavior. A model that fits every outcome predicted none of them.
H2 — ATTRIBUTION REVERSAL
Explain their behavior by circumstance, the way you explain your own.
Strong at
Correcting the default bias. More accurate than the untreated instinct, most of the time.
Structural flaw
Calibrated for one instance from a stranger. Run as a standing law, it hands repeat offenders a fresh excuse every time.
Combined output Every input resolves toward benign, situational, explicable. Comfort is the signal you're reading as accuracy.

03 / Resolution Map

Where the instrument is sharp, and where it is guessing
The skill is real. It is pointed backward.

Understanding and prediction feel like the same faculty from the inside. They aren't, and the first does not imply the second. Rapport is evidence that you modeled someone accurately in the moment; it is not evidence that you know what they'll do on Thursday.

04 / Operating Rules

Corrections, not replacements — keep H1 and H2
Disposition

One instance is situation. A pattern across independent contexts is character. Independent is load-bearing: late to the same meeting three times is one context; late to meetings, late on invoices, late on a favor is three. Refusing to name character at that point isn't fairness, it's a leak.

Silence

No reply is the reading, not the absence of one. Charity requires positive evidence, not merely the lack of contrary evidence. Default interpretation of a non-response: the offer didn't land. Overturn it with something, not with a story.

Falsifier

No read is finished until it emits something that could fail. "He's blocked by his boss" is a story. "If so, he'll ask for something forwardable and won't raise price" is a read. Only the second one can pay you back.

Depth

Two levels of recursion, held loosely. Past that, accuracy collapses and only the sensation of insight remains — which is indistinguishable from the real thing while you're inside it.

Ask

The highest-accuracy instrument available is a question. It stays underused because inferring silently feels more sophisticated than checking. It isn't; it's just cheaper to be wrong that way.

Ship

The unit of progress is a person who said yes or no. Not a repo that runs. A new directory is the most comfortable possible way to not find out.
