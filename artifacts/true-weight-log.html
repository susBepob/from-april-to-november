A single scale reading moves ±1 kg a day with water, salt and food, so it isn't your weight. The mean of recent readings is the better estimate, and the ± figure shows how precise that estimate is (95% range). Short windows react to real change faster; long windows are steadier. Weigh at the same time each day, ideally in the morning.
