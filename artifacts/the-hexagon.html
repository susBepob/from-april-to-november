      The three rules

      1. Seven touches a day. Every cell, including the English centre, gets something daily, even 10 minutes on a bad day. A day where all seven got something counts as complete.
      2. Weekly shape check. On Sunday, score each corner 0–5. Aim for an even shape, not one long spike.
      3. Seasons change which corners get the most time. The corners marked WIN get the most time this phase. The others still get their daily touch.

      Shared walls for English
